About Fortressed

Fortressed is a strategy and action game currently under development. Players must build powerful bases, defend their territory, gather resources, and defeat enemies through tactical gameplay.

The project will feature multiple game modes, each offering a unique experience.

Game Mode 1 - Base Siege

Base Siege is a competitive mode where players build strong fortresses and attack enemy bases.

  • Build walls and defenses.
  • Upgrade your base.
  • Train powerful units.
  • Launch attacks against enemy fortresses.
  • Destroy the enemy's headquarters to win.

Game Mode 2 - Tribe Defence

Tribe Defence is a survival mode focused on protecting your tribe from waves of enemies.

  • Gather food, wood, and stone.
  • Expand your village.
  • Recruit warriors and hunters.
  • Defend against increasingly difficult attacks.
  • Keep your tribe alive as long as possible.

Enchanting Table

Your XP level: 0

Tap an item in your bag to put it in the slot (tap the slot to take it back). Swords get Sharpness (more damage), armor gets Protection (less damage taken), tools get Efficiency (mine faster), pickaxes/axes also Fortune (more ore / wood drops), and axes can also take Sharpness. Craft a shield (planks + iron) to block frontal attacks — axes can temporarily disable a raised shield. Swords can also take Looting (I–V, more XP and more drops from kills). Sharpness goes up to V (25 levels). Bows can take Power (I–V, arrow damage), Charge (I–II, arrow knockback) and Infinity (25 levels, shooting doesn't use up arrows; you still need one in your bag). A bow (3 sticks + 3 string at a crafting table; 1 wool = 4 string) shoots arrows (iron + stick + string = 4): hold right-click to draw, release to fire. Every tool, sword, bow and armor piece can also take Unbreaking (I–III, lasts longer) and Mending (I, XP you earn repairs your held item, off-hand and worn armor). Enchant costs: level I = 5, II = 10, III = 15, IV = 20 XP levels (Mending = 25). Kill mobs to earn XP · Press E to close

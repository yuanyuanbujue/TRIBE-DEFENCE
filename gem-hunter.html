How to Play

Move: Arrow keys or WASD to walk around the map.

Goal: Collect every gem 💎 scattered across the map.

Danger: Red guards patrol the map. Getting touched costs a life and gives you a short burst of invincibility.

Minimap: Check the top-right corner to see gems, guards, and your position.

Tip: Use walls to break line of sight from Alert and Hunt guards.

Money: Gems collected earn you money 💰. Spend it on skins, and on the two Dealers below.

Difficulty Bonus Gems: The higher the difficulty, the more gems you earn. At max Game Difficulty (Hacker), each gem collected is worth up to 5 gems instead of 1. The higher your Guard Difficulty (the guard behavior unlocked/selected — Wander → Alert → Hunt), the bigger a one-time bonus of extra gems you get added to your account once the game ends, up to +5 gems at Hunt (the hardest).

Gems → $GIT: Gems earned this way go into your gem balance first. The 💱 Converter (on the main menu) lets you turn gems into $GIT — but it's locked until you earn the "WOW U R NOW RICH" achievement by collecting 100 gems in a single run!

🏆 Achievements: Check the Achievements button (above ⚙️ Settings) to see which achievements you've earned and which are still locked. Every achievement you unlock grants an instant +50 gem bonus.

🔫 Weapon Dealer (Step 3): Pay 10,000💰 once to unlock the Weapon Dealer's shop. Weapons stay hidden until you do. Once unlocked, weapons must be bought in order — Speed Boots → Shield → Phase Blink → Aegis Crown — you can't buy or equip one until you own the weapon before it.

😈 Stage 2 — Purple Guards: Gems are now unlimited — keep collecting as long as you survive! Once you collect 30 gems in a run, faster purple guards join the hunt and relentlessly chase you no matter what. Only an 🧪 Invisibility Potion can save you from them.

🔨 Stage 3 — Blue Mace Guards: Clear out every purple guard and a fresh wave spawns — the original number of red guards, more purple guards, and blue guards armed with maces. Blue guards have more HP than purple guards, chase you more cleverly (they lead your movement instead of just following), and are only a little faster. Before slamming down, a blue guard fires a fireball beneath its own feet to rocket-boost itself into the air — that's launch propulsion, not an attack on its own, so watch where the guard actually lands instead. While airborne a guard moves 89% slower, so use that window to get clear before the mace-smash comes down.

👑 Stage 4 — Elite Guards: Clear every blue guard and the elite guards arrive — tougher, faster mace guards whose maces are enchanted (shown by their glowing purple aura), hitting harder than a regular mace-smash. Waves keep reinforcing endlessly from here.

🧪 Invisibility Potions: Buy them from the Weapon Dealer's shop (Step 3) for 💰250 each, once unlocked. Use your Drink Potion key (default P, rebindable in ⚙️ Settings) during a run to turn invisible to all guards for a few seconds.

🕴️ Guard Dealer (Step 4): Guards default to Alert. Pay the Guard Dealer 10,000💰 to permanently unlock one random guard behavior. This is a separate dealer from the Weapon Dealer, with its own 10,000💰 fee each time you use it, and it can be paid repeatedly until every behavior is unlocked.

How to Play

Move: Arrow keys or WASD to walk around the map.

Goal: Collect every gem 💎 scattered across the map.

Danger: Red guards patrol the map. Getting touched costs a life and gives you a short burst of invincibility.

Minimap: Check the top-right corner to see gems, guards, and your position.

Tip: Use walls to break line of sight from Alert and Hunt guards.

Money: Gems collected earn you money. Spend it on skins, and buy weapons from the Weapon Dealer on Step 3.

⛏ TRIBE DEFENCE ⚔

Goal

Mine trees and rocks for wood and stone. Dig deeper into the wilds for iron, gold and diamond ore (an iron pickaxe or better is needed for diamonds). Iron and gold ore must be smelted into bars at a Furnace (requires a Workbench first, and costs 8 stone to build) before they can be used — stone and diamond are used raw. Build walls, upgrade your towers, pickaxe, sword and armor, and swing your sword to survive the night. How many days can you last?

Controls

  • WASD / Arrow keys — move
  • Number keys or click a slot — select a tool/item
  • Drag a hotbar slot onto another — swap their positions to rearrange your hotbar
  • Left click — mine/attack (auto), build, or break a placed block/tower directly under the cursor
  • Right click — open a chest or furnace, craft/upgrade/build at a workbench, or choose Upgrade/Break on a tower
  • Furnace and Chest must be built from the Workbench panel (right-click a workbench) — everything else stays on the 9-slot hotbar (keys 1-9), locked until you've built a workbench
  • Hold Shift — block with your Shield (once crafted; works offhand regardless of which slot is selected)
  • E — open Inventory, Armor & Equipment
  • C — Press C To Eat

Day and Night

Days are safe — no mobs spawn, so use the daylight to gather wood, stone and ore, build up your base, and craft gear. Once night falls, mobs start spawning and keep coming until dawn. Small mobs are weaker but numerous; big mobs hit harder and have far more HP, so gear up and fortify before the sun goes down.

Leaderboard & Chat

Submit your score (days survived + kills) from the 🏆 Leaderboard panel any time. Only the top 3 submitted scores can post in the "Top 3" chat tab.

Biomes

Survive to Day 50 and Day 100 to trigger a border expansion into tougher new biomes with stronger enemies.

📖 Wiki — Bestiary

Mobs only spawn at night, but any that are still alive at dawn start burning in sunlight and take steady damage until they die or night falls again — except The Draugur, which is immune to daylight and never burns. Bigger, tougher variants become more common the longer you survive, and every biome beyond the Wilds brings faster, harder-hitting versions of the same threats. Note: a mob that burns to death without ever being hit by you or a turret gives no kill credit or points. Watch out — if a burning mob hits you, you'll catch fire too and take damage for 5 seconds unless you're standing in water.

🌲 The Wilds (Day 1+)

Wildling Skulker
Small · Common · Red
The most common night-time threat. Individually weak with modest HP and damage, but they spawn often and swarm in numbers — dangerous mainly if you let several surround you at once.
Wildling Brute
Big · Uncommon · Dark Red
A hulking, slower-moving variant with roughly three times the health and damage of a Skulker. Starts appearing from Day 3 onward and becomes steadily more frequent the longer a run goes. Worth prioritising with towers before it closes the distance.

🔥 Scorched Frontier (Day 50+)

Ember Skulker
Small · Common · Orange
Found beyond the expanded border after surviving to Day 50. Faster and hits noticeably harder than a Wildling Skulker, reflecting the tougher biome it calls home.
Ember Brute
Big · Uncommon · Dark Orange
A significant step up from a Wildling Brute in both health and damage, and appears more often than its Wilds counterpart. Upgraded towers and armor are strongly recommended before pushing this far.

🌀 Void Reaches (Day 100+)

Void Skulker
Small · Common · Purple
The fastest and hardest-hitting Skulker variant, roaming the outermost ring unlocked after Day 100. Still individually weaker than any Brute, but their speed makes them harder to kite.
Void Brute
Big · Uncommon · Dark Purple
The deadliest mob currently in the game — very high health and damage on top of the Void Reaches' speed bonus. Treat these as top priority targets for your strongest turrets.

💀 Special Mobs

The Draugur
Rare · Any biome · Day 11+ only
A ghostly undead that can appear in any biome once you've survived past Day 10. Rather than chipping away with quick hits like other mobs, it strikes slowly for a heavy ~21 damage and, on contact, shatters your shield ward — disabling blocking for a few seconds. Its attack has a 5 second cooldown, so it hits hard but infrequently. Unlike every other mob, it's immune to daylight and never burns, so it can linger well past dawn. Easy to spot by its pale teal glow and skull marking; worth killing at range with turrets before it gets close.

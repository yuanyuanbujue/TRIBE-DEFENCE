How To Play

Goal: the shotgun is loaded with a random mix of LIVE and BLANK shells. Take turns shooting yourself or the dealer until one of you runs out of hearts.

Blanks deal no damage, and if you shoot yourself with one, you get to go again. Live shells deal damage and end your turn.

Items: each new wave you and the dealer both draw 2 random items. 🔍 peek the next shell, 🚬 heal 1 HP, 🍺 rack out the current shell, ⛓️ make your opponent skip a turn, 🪚 double your next live shot's damage, 🔄 flip the chambered shell live⇄blank, 🥷 steal a random item from your opponent, 💊 expired medicine — a gamble to heal 2 HP or lose 1. Using an item doesn't end your turn. You can now see what items the dealer is holding.

Endless: optional mode, toggle it on the difficulty screen. Beat the dealer and a tougher one steps up next wave — survive as many waves as you can. In Default mode, the match just ends when someone wins.

In Tutorial mode, the dealer plays a lot more carelessly so you can get a feel for the game.

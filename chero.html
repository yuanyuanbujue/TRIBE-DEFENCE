CHERO

Protect your King. A tall board. Not chess.

How to Play

  1. Two players share one device. Pink picks first and moves first from the bottom. Blue plays from the top.
  2. Each player picks 15 hieroglyphs. Every hieroglyph is a piece, and the small mark on it shows its type (see the table). Your King is always included.
  3. Tap one of your pieces. Yellow dots show where it can go.
  4. Tap a dot to move. A yellow ring means you can capture a piece there.
  5. Players take turns, one move each.
  6. Protect your King (👑). If it is captured, you lose the game.
  7. Capture the other player's King to win.
  8. Magic: if you picked the Mage ✨, press Cast magic instead of moving. Craft a true Egyptian magic word from the Spellbook, using hieroglyphs you picked that are still alive. Unknown words have no power. Then follow the prompt to aim the spell.

Your pieces

👑KingSteps 1 square in any direction.
✚RunnerSlides up to 4 squares straight (up, down, left, right). Cannot jump.
✖GuardSlides up to 2 squares diagonally. Cannot jump.
◆LeaperJumps exactly 2 squares in any of 8 directions, over anything.
●PawnSteps 1 forward (2 on its first move). Captures 1 square diagonally forward.
✨MageSteps 1 square in any direction. While it is alive, you can cast a magic word. After a cast, wait 3 of your own turns before casting again.

Rules

  • The board is 9 squares wide and 18 squares tall, black and white.
  • Before the game, each player picks 15 of the 26 alphabet hieroglyphs. Each one is a fixed piece type: 👑 King, ✚ Runner, ✖ Guard, ◆ Leaper, ● Pawn, ✨ Mage. The King hieroglyph is always one of your 15.
  • Pieces are placed in the order you picked them: King in the middle of the back row, then the rest of the back row, then the front row.
  • You must move one piece every turn. There is no passing.
  • Moving onto an enemy piece captures it. You cannot land on your own pieces.
  • Runners and Guards are blocked by any piece in their path. Leapers are never blocked.
  • A Pawn that reaches the far end of the board becomes a Runner.
  • Magic: if your Mage ✨ is alive and your magic is ready, press Cast magic instead of moving. After a cast you must wait 3 of your own turns before you can cast again. Energy: every spell drains your ⚡ energy bar (the cost is shown in the Spellbook) and you regain a little each turn. If a cast empties your bar you burn out and lose. Craft a word from the Spellbook below, using only hieroglyphs you picked whose pieces are still alive. A word that is not in the Spellbook has no power and fizzles (see Modes).
  • Modes: Easy: your Spellbook lists the spells you can make and crafts them when you tap. Normal: the Spellbook is a reference, you craft each word yourself, and a wrong word fizzles at no cost. Hard: no Spellbook during play, and a wrong word wastes your cast and your turn.
  • When your King can be captured on the next move, it glows red and the status says it is in danger. The game will not stop you from leaving it there.
  • Goal: keep your King alive. The game ends the moment a King is captured, and that player loses.

Local mode is pass-and-play on one device. Online mode matches you against another player over the network — create a room and share the code, or join a friend's code. Both battle on a full 9×10 Xiangqi-style board with a river and palace. Capture the enemy Leader (glowing piece) or wipe out all their pieces to win. Buff rules: Chess gets an extra Pawn + Queen vs Xiangqi. Checkers gets a third full row of pieces (every square filled, not just dark squares) whenever it faces Chess or Xiangqi — its baseline is 2 full rows. Mirror matchups get no buffs. Casual mashup ruleset: no check/checkmate restriction, no castling/en passant, checkers allows chain jumps. Use Customize above to give each board type its own color theme, and give each player their own piece colors.

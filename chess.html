Choose Your Chess Type

Local mode is pass-and-play on one device. Online mode matches you against another player over the network — create a room and share the code, or join a friend's code. Both battle on a full 9×10 Xiangqi-style board with a river and palace. Capture the enemy Leader (glowing piece) or wipe out all their pieces to win. Buff rules: Chess gets an extra Pawn + Queen vs Xiangqi or Shogi. Checkers gets a third full row of pieces (every square filled, not just dark squares) whenever it faces Chess or Xiangqi — its baseline is 2 full rows. Mirror matchups get no buffs. Rules: Chess has castling, en passant & promotion; Chess, Xiangqi (incl. flying-general) and Shogi enforce check, checkmate and stalemate; Shogi pieces promote in the last 3 rows (you choose to promote or not), and the Shogi player's captured pieces — of any variant, except Leaders — switch sides and can be dropped on any empty square from your hand, facing your opponent; every variant lets you choose what to promote to: chess pawns (Queen/Rook/Bishop/Knight), Xiangqi soldiers reaching the last rank (Chariot/Horse/Cannon) and checkers men reaching the last row (King or Queen/Rook/Bishop/Knight); checkers allows chain jumps. Classic warns about check; Hardcore never warns, never enforces check — you lose only when your King is captured. Right-click (or long-press) any piece to see its move range (blue), attack range (orange) and blocked squares it could reach if the way were clear (purple dashed diamonds). Use Customize above to give each board type its own color theme, and give each player their own piece colors.
